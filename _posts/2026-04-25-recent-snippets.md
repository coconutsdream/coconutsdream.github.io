april and may are tulips season in the netherlands. happy to have h's friends from dublin visiting ❤️🌷

also it was my first time trying the fujifilm xt-50 with an xf16-50mm, f2.8-4.8 zoom lens. the zoom lens is of course much more versatile and especially really great for portraits, but still getting used to using the xt-50, or rather exploring how to use the xt-50 to its full capabilities. 

i find that the custom film simulation dials are somewhat confusing, as they do over-ride the saved simulation settings. it is therefore not very intuitive to use if you want to shoot a quick straight-out-of-camera "lifestyle" type photography. the zoom lens also can be a little "distracting" as comes with various focal lengths, so sometimes i find myself becoming less intentional with the composition of the images, and starting to fiddle around with the different lengths of shooting. 

but of course, maybe it's just because i'm trying it out for the first time. overall i do like the xt-50. it is lightweight (438grams), and with the lens it becomes a very nice carry-around camera.
