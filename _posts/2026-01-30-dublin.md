first time to dublin. i had the best steak in my life at [f.x. bickley steakhouse](https://maps.app.goo.gl/N254knSPthZgmcHy7) on pembroke street. the guiness tasted amazing as well. the smoothness, the just-right bitterness kick .. oh it was incredible. the city was cool, but not the traffic and the weather.

we spent a lovely weekend at h's friend's who also owns a british shorthair. the meow's name was toasty and he would not stop playing with the toys! he could even do tricks as well, which was super cute...

quite honetly, i've had series of unfortunate events up to this trip (and during) to dublin. first it was the rejections followed by the intense chase of more application deadlines all due at the end of this month. then there was the accident on spilling water on my macbook pro, which broke instantly and was irrecoverable.

right after this, h's friend's unruly samoyed knocked my beloved fujifilm x100vi in dublin...the sensor broke and the camera was left with an ugly dent. the dog was cute ... but it was way too energetic, unruly and straightup wild. my broken camera left a huge dent in my heart 💔

but, in midst of all these chaos, i found a penny on the ground in dublin. then i picked up it, placed it in my pocket, and decided that this is *luck* from the universe, and all these shitty events must stop from now on 🍀

i think the universe heard my cries. because after this trip, magic and good things started happening ...
